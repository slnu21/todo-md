# TODO.md v0.5.0 — export · terms of use

## New
- **Export**: Settings ▸ Data folder ▸ **Export**. Saves all to-dos and memos as one JSON file, including completions older than 30 days (archive files), with project names, status, importance, due date, owner, start/done times, sub-items and the memo log. This file format (`todo-md-export` v1) stays stable even if the app's internal storage changes.

## Changed
- **Terms of use and privacy policy** are in place. TODO.md is **free** for personal and business use; your to-dos and memos are yours. No personal information is collected and nothing is sent anywhere. See `EULA.md` and `privacy.md` in the zip.
- Ready for the Microsoft Store edition: when installed from the Store, the data folder lives in an app-private location, so Settings shows that actual path and "Open folder" opens it. "Launch at sign-in" is available in the portable edition only.
